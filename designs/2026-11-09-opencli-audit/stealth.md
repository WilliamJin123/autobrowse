## 2. Do not copy

- **The JS stealth patch (`O:stealth.ts`).** Each patched property is its own tell. Our D3 decision and the `webdriver` comment at `A:session.ts:213` say the same.
- **Anti-`debugger` and Error.stack overrides.** They are detectable, and we don't leak a debugger transport the way an extension does.
- **`Input.insertText` / instant clicks as the default (`O:base-page.ts:1293-1296`, `:1270`).** They produce no key events and a teleporting pointer.
- **Visibility gate on `document.visibilityState` (`O:base-page.ts:43,360`).** Our headed Chrome keeps a visible window instead (`A:session.ts:270-275`).
- **Delegating captcha to the user's browser (`O:clis/ctrip/attraction.js:52`).** We already solve in-flow.
- **Env-var slot pool (`O:execution.ts:623`).** Our caps ledger and `limits.json` do the same, per account and persisted.
- **Strategy labels (PUBLIC, COOKIE, UI) as risk metadata.** They are metadata only and carry no safety behavior.
- **Running in the user's real Chrome as the isolation model.** The shared IP and profile are the risk we avoid.

## 3. Top three upgrades, in order

1. **Ban-signal engine.** One per-site table of suspended / restricted / "unusual activity" / checkpoint / site-429 patterns, wired into `caps.hold` and the 1/3/8/12 h steps (`A:facade.ts:879-888`, `A:flows/proof-shot.ts:23`). Today it is only an "undone act" signal. M.
2. **Warm-up age ramp and the explore cap guard.** `firstSeen` in `A:caps.ts` plus a `compiledDeps` guard at `A:workflows/compiled-deps.ts:40`, redone per site from real behavior (WA-1148). M and S.
3. **Window-level pacing and fail-closed hands.** Per-hour and quiet-hour limits in `A:caps.ts`, plus no `instantHands` on acts for personal or capped sites (`A:flow.ts:494`). M and S.
