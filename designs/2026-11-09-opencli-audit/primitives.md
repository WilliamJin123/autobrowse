## 2. Do not copy
- **`type` with instant `Input.insertText`** (`bp:1294`): this is the bot signal autobrowse's `human/` exists to avoid.
- **`data-opencli-ref` attributes left in the page** (`dom-snapshot.ts:682`): a detectable DOM mark. Use a temp attribute that gets cleared, as `marks.ts` does.
- **Eval and extract through the main world with no scriptGate** (`bt:1789`): bypasses the payment and send gates.
- **Persisted network bodies in `~/.opencli/cache`** (`network-cache.ts:14`): conflicts with autobrowse's redaction policy.
- **`chrome call` raw method access** (`bt:838`): no gate on what it touches.
- **The AX prototype path** (`bt:692`, `--source ax`): OpenCLI itself calls it unproven; Playwright `ariaSnapshot` already covers it.
- **`wait time N` as a primary wait** (`bt:1712`): `settle` and locator auto-wait are better.

## 3. Top three upgrades
1. **Dialog handling** (`ss` plus `commands.ts`). An unhandled `confirm` or `beforeunload` silently auto-dismisses today. It is the only real correctness hole in this facet.
2. **Numbered `state` with live-DOM cost ceiling** (`commands.ts`, `ss`, `marks.ts`). One call returns a numbered, scroll-aware, occlusion-filtered list, with stale-mark rematching. It cuts the aria round trips.
3. **`wait` plus click-triggered download capture** (`commands.ts`, `ss:2496`). Gives deterministic barriers and covers JS-triggered downloads.
