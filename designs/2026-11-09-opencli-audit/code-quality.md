**Do not copy**
- Plain-JS adapters outside the compiler (`clis/*.js`): this is why OC needs convention-audit at all.
- `Arg.type?: string` plus `CommandArgs = Record&lt;string, any&gt;`: zod schemas are strictly better.
- `IPage` as a 60-method surface with a third of the methods optional: it leaks transport differences into adapters.
- Hand-copied clamp helpers (660 hits): `silent-clamp` and the 130-entry baseline show the cost.
- Baseline-ratchet lint files (`scripts/*-baseline.json`): fine as a tactic, but it records debt rather than fixing it.
- `globalThis.__opencli_registry__` singleton (`registry.ts:131-134`): our one-door import lint already avoids duplicate module instances.
- Wide error-envelope patching in `command-output.ts:25-49`: it copies fields off arbitrary error objects by key name. That is duck-typed and fragile.

**Three highest-value upgrades, in order**
1. Add a zod `response` schema to `SiteRoute`, validate it in the facade for both legs, and remove the `route()` erasure (`src/sites/types.ts:70,341`, `src/sites/facade.ts:1004-1014`).
2. Add one shared error contract, `{code, hint, retryable, outcome}`, mapped from `SiteError`, `AccessRefused` and `FlowFailed` at the facade edge (new `src/errors.ts`, called from `facade.ts`).
3. Add a convention audit for routes and flows (static checks on the route listing, like OC's `convention-audit.ts`) in the gates, plus a route scaffold command.
