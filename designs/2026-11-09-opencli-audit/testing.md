## 2. Do not copy

- **`tryBrowserCommand` silent skip** (`O:browser-public.test.ts:14-31`): a bot wall or a missing bridge passes the test as green. OpenCLI's own doc says so (`O:testing.md:59-61`).
- **Dead `validateRowShape` with its 12-key cap** (`O:verify-fixture.ts:263`): it isn't wired into `adapter test`.
- **Fixtures in `~/.opencli/sites/...`** (`O:verify-fixture.ts:110-112`): they live outside the repo and nothing versions them. Ours would sit beside each workflow.
- **`eval-skill` self-judging** (`O:eval-skill.ts:105-110`): the agent grades its own output. Use deterministic checks first, then a separate judge.
- **`eval-*` tasks on third-party sites with no signed-in or money guard**: our evals must keep the payment and send gates on.
- **Per-command `createPageMock` call assertions** (`O:clis/test-utils.js:10`): they pin call order, not behaviour.
- **Bun and Windows jobs**: we are macOS and Linux with patchright.
- **Heuristic regex convention rules** (`O:convention-audit.ts`): the clamp and sentinel rules fit row-returning adapters, not our flows. Port the idea, not the rules.

## 3. Top three upgrades

1. Weekly scheduled `site-probe` plus a re-prove of every compiled workflow, with typed failures. It adds a `schedule:` to `ci.yml`, reuses `src/workflows/proof.ts`, and feeds `autobrowse success`.
2. Proof `expect` fixtures (`rowCount`, `types`, `patterns`, `notEmpty`, `mustNotContain`, `mustBeTruthy`), seeded from the first good proof. They turn `proof.json` from "ran" into "returned the right shape".
3. An in-repo `evals/` suite with a judge, train/test split and baseline, run through `agent --json`. Bench and a prompt-tuning loop come after it.
